a great day in three moves

1

build it stop by stop

real-place search for every stop: the coffee, the gallery, the bar. bop lines them up in walking order.

2

live it as you go

start your bop when you head out. every arrival gets a stamp, every photo lands at the right stop.

3

share the whole day

post it to your story or send one link with every stop, time, and place on a map.
